## Brand & Style

This design system targets high-achieving mentors, educators, and executive thought leaders who demand an elevated personal presence. The aesthetic blends dark editorial authority with high-velocity product execution: deep abyssal midnight blues and obsidian violet bases punctuated by crisp white content surfaces, vibrant royal blue action points, and subtle luminous violet glows.

The personality balances prestige, focus, and modern academic mastery. The user experience evokes a curated modern academy—restrained yet vibrant, tech-forward yet deeply grounded in typography and structure. Visual movements draw from a hybrid of **Modern Editorial** and **Glass-tinted Minimalist Structure**, leveraging crisp content containers, subtle gradient hair lines, and deliberate optical contrast to highlight intellectual caliber.

## Colors

The color palette operates primarily in a dark mode context, relying on intense, deep midnight-violet (`#0D0016`) and deep navy bases (`#060814`). These deep dark foundations create an atmosphere of focus and luxury.

Key color roles:
- **Primary (`#2563eb`)**: High-potency Royal Blue used for prominent interactive states, key CTA triggers, conversion targets, and focus states.
- **Secondary (`#8b5cf6`)**: Electric Violet, providing an intellectual, sleek accent for badges, mentorship tier indicators, tag highlights, and atmospheric radial glows.
- **Tertiary (`#38bdf8`)**: Electric Sky Cyan, reserved for metrics, verified educator ticks, active session markers, and fine data visuals.
- **Neutral (`#0D0016`)**: Rich violet-black canvas foundation that anchors deep surfaces.
- **Contrast White Cards (`surface-card-light: #FFFFFF`)**: Used selectively for featured mentorship tiers, masterclass spotlight modules, and application popouts to create high-impact editorial contrast against the dark midnight canvas.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a dynamic pairing of **Montserrat** for display headers and **Manrope** for analytical text, metadata, and editorial body narrative. 

- **Montserrat** delivers geometric impact, confidence, and punchy editorial gravity. Titles are set tightly with negative letter spacing to give titles an architectural presence.
- **Manrope** introduces contemporary legibility, technical precision, and humanist warmth for long-form curriculum details, mentor biographies, and UI metadata.
- For micro-tags, tracking metrics, and cohort dates, `label-xs` is rendered in uppercase with wide tracking (`0.08em`) to mimic prestige editorial publication captions.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform layout follows a structured 12-column grid system on desktop (`1280px` max-content envelope) designed with generous negative space to ensure an unhurried, editorial reading cadence:
- **Desktop (>=1024px)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` boundary margin. Masterclass schedules and mentor dossiers expand across structured asymmetric grids (e.g., 5-column sticky bio alongside a 7-column timeline).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` screen margins. Multi-column cards collapse to 2-up layouts.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen margins. Visual structures stack linearly with deliberate vertical separation (`space-xl`) between cohorts and modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined interplay of deep ambient lighting, hairline borders, and contrasting bright surfaces:

1. **Base Layer (Canvas)**: Deep midnight obsidian (`#060814` to `#0D0016`) enriched by wide, soft radial background lighting gradients (e.g., violet and royal blue blurs at 15–20% opacity with `120px` blur radius) that emerge beneath key credentials and CTAs.
2. **Subtle Dark Layer (Dark Surface)**: Elements sit on `#13132B` with a `1px` crisp hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or `#202142`), eliminating heavy box shadows in favor of crisp architectural contouring.
3. **Elevated Pop Layer (Crisp White Inverted Cards)**: High-priority modules (e.g., enrollment checkout, key speaker badges, featured book launches) flip into clean, stark white containers (`#FFFFFF`) featuring charcoal-black text (`#090A1A`). These are anchored with deep multi-layered shadows (`0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`) to establish instant visual dominance over the dark atmosphere.
4. **Interactive Hover Elevation**: Hovered dark cards elevate via soft primary ambient glow (`0 8px 30px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.25)`) and slight border illumination.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined balance between modern software ergonomics and sleek editorial framing by utilizing **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature:
- Standard buttons, input surfaces, and module cards receive `0.5rem` (8px) corners.
- Featured profile modules and standout presentation cards scale to `1rem` (16px) or `1.5rem` (24px).
- Badges, status chips, and avatar highlights retain a full circular pill profile to balance the geometric cards.
- Hairline borders (`1px`) frame geometric edges with surgical accuracy.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid royal blue (`#2563eb`) with crisp white text (`#ffffff`), `0.5rem` radius, subtle top-edge bevel shine (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`), and an animated violet hover transition.
- **Secondary (Dark Surface)**: Translucent midnight background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), `1px` border in `#202142`, shifting to clean white text on hover with a faint royal blue halo.
- **Inverted Card Button**: Solid black (`#090A1A`) button with white typography and subtle hover scale inside white card modules.

### Cards
- **Dark Dossier Card**: Background `#13132B`, `1px` border `#202142`, `1rem` roundedness, padded with `1.5rem`. Used for curriculum syllabi, student outcomes, and mentor testimonials.
- **High-Impact White Card**: Solid `#FFFFFF`, intense drop shadow, jet-black typography (`#090A1A`), paired with blue badge overlays for featured masterclasses and tier selections.

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Category Chips**: Small, pill-shaped (`9999px`), `0.75rem` vertical padding, font `label-xs` in uppercase. Violet tinted backgrounds (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.12)`) framed with `1px` violet stroke (`#8b5cf6`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Dark slate base (`#0E1022`), `1px` border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, dynamic royal blue glow outline (`#2563eb`) upon `:focus`. Placeholder rendered in muted slate (`#64748b`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom square/round shapes with dark navy background, expanding into solid royal blue with crisp white checks on active selection.

### Custom Platform Modules
- **Mentor Credential Pill**: Dark glass pill displaying live status indicator (pulsing tertiary cyan `#38bdf8` dot) alongside follower or student counts.
- **Stat Metric Block**: Giant `display` numeral in white paired with an electric blue or violet horizontal hairline accent bar and a muted `label-md` descriptive title below.
